Simple period calendar for your iPhone home screen.

Track period dates, see estimated ovulation and fertile window highlights, and check your current cycle from a clean home screen widget.

Built for quick cycle checks

MiniCycle keeps the monthly view, daily records, predictions, and widget visible without filling the app with unrelated features.

  • Period start and end tracking
  • Estimated period, ovulation, and fertile window dates
  • Recent-record statistics for cycle and period length
  • Local by default, optional iCloud Sync
