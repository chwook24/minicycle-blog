How to keep your period tracker records when moving to a new iPhone

Published May 23, 2026·by Chanwook Kim, MiniCycle developer·Updated November 12, 2026·4 min read

At a glance

Keep the old iPhone until you can open your saved period dates and notes in MiniCycle on the new one. Records are stored locally by default, so prepare MiniCycle's optional iCloud Sync or an Apple device transfer or backup restore before changing phones. If you use MiniCycle iCloud Sync, check it on the old phone first, then enable it in MiniCycle Settings on the new phone using the same Apple Account (Apple ID).

Start with the right expectation

MiniCycle's optional iCloud Sync and Apple's iCloud device backup are separate settings. MiniCycle uses CloudKit to keep a copy of its records and settings in your private iCloud account, as described in the MiniCycle Privacy Policy. Signing in with the same Apple Account alone does not make the app fetch that copy.

  1. On the old iPhone: open MiniCycle and confirm your records are present. Before relying on MiniCycle iCloud Sync, check that it is enabled and shows a recent successful sync rather than an error.
  2. On the new iPhone: use the same Apple Account, open MiniCycle Settings, enable iCloud Sync, and check its status. An error needs attention before you treat the transfer as complete.
  3. Compare the records: check saved dates and notes on both phones. A successful status message is useful, but it does not replace comparing the history.

If each phone has records that the other does not, keep both copies and ask support before trying to reconcile them.

If you leave MiniCycle iCloud Sync off, choose Apple's device migration or backup restoration route for the phone's local data, then verify the result in MiniCycle.

Use Apple's transfer or backup flow

For Quick Start, Apple says to have Wi-Fi connected and Bluetooth on, and to keep both devices nearby and connected to power until migration finishes. Direct transfer and downloading from iCloud have different completion steps; follow the option you selected.

If using an iCloud device backup, check the last completed backup's date and time under Back Up Now. Apple's iCloud backup instructions explain how to make and confirm that backup. A backup from before your latest entries cannot show those later changes on its own.

If you use a computer backup, review Apple's computer-transfer instructions and encrypted-backup guidance. Current screens and requirements are documented by Apple.

Check MiniCycle before erasing the old iPhone

Compare saved entries, not just the next predicted period. For a fictional check, if the old phone shows a saved September 4–8 period with a note on September 5, open those same dates on the new phone and confirm both the interval and the note. Use your own known dates for the real check.

  • Compare the most recent saved period's start and end dates.
  • Open an older period to check that earlier history is present too.
  • Open a date with a known note and compare its contents.

Review statistics and the widget after the saved history matches. A predicted date or widget display alone does not establish that all records transferred.

Before using the reset control, read How to reset MiniCycle and erase all your period records.

If you are switching from another period app

MiniCycle does not currently import records automatically from other period-tracking apps. If the previous app offers an export, keep that file for your own records and manually enter the period starts you trust most.

A practical migration checklist

  1. Before moving: keep the old phone's records available, choose the transfer route, and confirm the relevant sync or completed backup.
  2. After setup: finish the chosen transfer process, open MiniCycle, and compare recent dates, an older period, and a known note.
  3. Before erasing or trading in: resolve missing entries first. Keep the old phone until you have verified the history you need on the new one.

If records are missing on the new iPhone

  • Records are still on the old phone: keep that copy. Do not use MiniCycle's Reset records control or delete the old app as a troubleshooting step.
  • You chose MiniCycle iCloud Sync: check the Apple Account on each phone, the old phone's sync status, and whether iCloud Sync is enabled inside MiniCycle on the new phone. Check any connection or iCloud error shown in the app.
  • You chose an Apple backup or transfer: check that the selected process has finished. Apple notes that content can continue downloading after setup; see its backup restoration guide. If you consider restoring an already configured new iPhone, read those steps first: an iCloud backup restore can require erasing that new phone, so preserve anything newly created there before proceeding.
  • Saved records are present, but only the widget looks wrong: follow MiniCycle widget refresh troubleshooting.

If history is still missing, keep the old phone and contact MiniCycle support with the app version, iOS version, and error message. You do not need to include private dates or note contents.
